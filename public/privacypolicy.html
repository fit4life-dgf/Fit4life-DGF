FIT4LIFE privacy

FIT4LIFE is the training app of your gym. This page explains how the app uses your health data.

What we read

With your permission, the app reads steps, heart rate, resting heart rate, sleep, calories, blood oxygen, respiration, heart-rate variability and weight from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS). You choose which types to allow and can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.

How it is used

The data is saved to your FIT4LIFE account so you can see your daily score, sleep and progress charts. It is visible to you and to the trainer and gym staff assigned to you. It is not sold and not used for advertising.

Where it is stored

In your gym's database on Supabase, protected by per-user access rules. Only signed-in users with the right role can read it.

Deleting your data

Ask your gym to delete your account and its data. You can also stop syncing by removing FIT4LIFE's access in Health Connect or Apple Health.

Not medical advice

The app and its AI coach give general fitness guidance and are not a substitute for a doctor.
